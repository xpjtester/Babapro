About BabaPro

By Qi · September 24, 2026

BabaPro is the name of my game project.

I created BabaPro because I hope to make a game that my sons would enjoy playing.

Or, well... at least a game they don't dislike. :)

That's the whole idea behind BabaPro: make small games, keep them fun, and build something I'd be happy to put in front of my own kids.

— Qi
