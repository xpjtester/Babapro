About BabaPro

By Qi · September 24, 2026
Family photo

BabaPro is the name of my game project.

I created BabaPro because I want to make a game that my sons would actually enjoy playing.

Well... maybe “enjoy” is setting the bar a little too high.

If I can make something they don't hate, I'll call that a pretty good start. :)

That's the whole idea behind BabaPro: make small games, keep them fun, and build something I'd be happy to put in front of my own kids.

— Qi
